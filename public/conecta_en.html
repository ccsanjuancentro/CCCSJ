Bookstore Ministry

The Bookstore Ministry was born in 2006 as an initiative to encourage reading and provide literary resources to the congregation. Since its inception, it has been a space dedicated to the sale of Bibles, Christian literature, children's material, notebooks, among other items. In addition, the ministry has a Library that has served as a valuable resource for those seeking spiritual growth and knowledge.

Over the years, the Bookstore Ministry has faced challenges; however, we remain committed to our mission of offering quality literature.

We invite the congregation and the community to support and visit the Bookstore Ministry, whether to purchase books, donate materials, or simply enjoy the Library. Every contribution and visit is essential to keeping this initiative alive, which has blessed so many people.

Bonds of Love

Wardrobe

The Bonds of Love Ministry was born with the mission of providing support to those who need it most, especially children in remote departments like Tupeli, Caucete, Cochagual, Silvica, and Pagancillo. Through our campaigns, we not only share the gospel message but also distribute clothing and reach out to various families.

We invite everyone to collaborate by donating clean clothing, in good condition and prepared for delivery. Each garment must be presented neatly and ready to be handed out.

This ministry is led by Rosita Massut and Carina Infante, with the collaboration of many women from the church. They carry out a valuable task washing and preparing each garment for distribution.

We deeply appreciate the effort and commitment of all the women who form part of this beautiful work, being an instrument of blessing for so many families.

Granary

The Granary Ministry was born with the purpose of gathering non-perishable food to bless people going through difficult times, both church members and others who, experiencing homelessness, have come seeking help.

It is carried out through a list of brothers and sisters willing to donate. If you wish to be part of this network of generosity, we invite you to consult with sisters Elina and Susana.

There is no established list of people to donate to; instead, we pray to God to show us with wisdom who to deliver these goods to.

We encourage you to join this ministry and be part of this beautiful act of love and provision for those who need it most.

Temple Guards

Ushers Ministry

We are a fundamental ministry in the life of our church, because through our service we guarantee order, hospitality, and attention during services and special events. Our work consists of receiving with love every brother, sister, or person who arrives for the first time, giving them a warm welcome, answering their questions, and guiding them on whom they can talk to. We do everything with love and respect.

Additionally, during meetings, we serve by maintaining order and staying attentive to any situation or need that arises. From guiding visitors to watching over everyone's safety, our service allows the congregation to worship without distractions. The Bible reminds us: "Serve one another humbly in love" (Galatians 5:13), and that is what motivates us every day. This ministry is carried out with great dedication and responsibility, always seeking the guidance of the Holy Spirit in everything we do.

Although our work sometimes goes unnoticed, we know it is a sacred calling. We do not just open doors; we prepare the hearts of the congregation to receive God.

We invite you to be part of this beautiful ministry!

Media

Sound, Projection, Lights & Broadcasting

We serve the Lord by carrying His message through digital tools. We believe that every technical detail prepares the environment for the congregation to worship God without distractions and allows the Word to reach more people.

Among what we do is:
  • Sound and lighting console
  • Projection of lyrics and screens
  • Production of video, image, and audio material for events and teachings
  • Live streaming of sermons
  • Website support for the church
  • Management of official social networks

You don't need to be a technical expert to join: we train you! If you have willingness, love for service, and a desire to learn, we welcome you with open arms!

WHO ARE WE?

We are Christian Community, San Juan Center, a church dedicated to sharing the love of Christ by supporting one another and seeking the salvation of those who do not yet know His transforming grace.

We believe in God as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit (1 John 5:7), and that the Bible has only one interpretation and meaning: the one God has given it in its proper context (2 Peter 1:20-21). We affirm that salvation is not obtained by human merits, but by faith in the redemptive work of Christ on the cross (Ephesians 2:8-9). Life transformation is the fruit of the Holy Spirit in us (Galatians 5:22), and Christian practice must be voluntary and focused on the glory of God, free from selfish motives (Colossians 3:23).
